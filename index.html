FIELD MANUAL / ABOUT

One pick. Four stratagems. No contradictions.

HELLDROP is an unofficial Helldivers 2 fan utility. Pick a primary, secondary or stratagem to guarantee, then let the generator assemble your remaining gear from currently available equipment.

WHAT THE RULES MEAN

• An Autocannon, Recoilless Rifle, Spear or any other backpack-fed support weapon uses both your support-weapon and backpack allowances.

• A Shield Generator Pack, Jump Pack or other backpack prevents a second backpack choice.

• Normal support weapons cannot be doubled up. Expendable support weapons can accompany another support weapon.

• Stratagems never repeat, and individual-slot rerolls recheck the entire deployment.

• Warbond, Superstore and special-item ownership are configured separately. Rewards from unselected Warbonds and unowned Superstore gear are excluded. If you have not purchased a reward with Medals, exclude it in Individual Gear.

Weapon renders and stratagem art are loaded from the community-supported Helldivers API, sourced from the Helldivers Wiki. A smaller built-in item pool works when the catalog is unavailable. All artwork belongs to its respective rights holders.
